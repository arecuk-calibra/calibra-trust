Questions

What laboratories ask us

Is Calibra certified to ISO/IEC 27001 or ISO 9001?

Not yet. We run the management system both standards require as part of our daily work; certification follows an internal audit and a management review. The certification body will be named here.

Is Calibra subject to NIS2?

No. Calibra Genomics does not qualify under NIS2 or the Czech Cybersecurity Act that implements it (Act No. 264/2025 Coll.): it applies to medium-sized and larger organizations. We meet its Art. 21 security measures anyway, within the scope of our ISO/IEC 27001 management system, so laboratories regulated under NIS2 can include us in their supplier assessment.

Do you hold patient data?

No patient names, identifiers, variant calls or clinical results. Calibra processes technical QC metrics under sample codes your laboratory controls, under a data processing agreement.

What happens if there is a security incident?

We follow a written incident procedure and tell the laboratories affected what happened, what it touched and what to do. Personal data breaches are reported to the authority within 72 hours where required.
